Flips the sign of the |1⟩ component without changing basis-state probabilities.

4. Deutsch's Algorithm

Given a mystery function f(x), is it constant (always the same output) or balanced (equal 0s and 1s)? Classically you need two evaluations. Quantumly: one.

6. Qubit Technologies

Different physical systems can realise qubits — each with different trade-offs.

❄️ Superconducting

Microwave circuits of Josephson junctions. Fastest gates; used by IBM and Google.

⚡ Trapped Ion

Ions suspended in electromagnetic fields, manipulated with lasers. Very long coherence; used by IonQ, Quantinuum.

💡 Photonic

Qubits encoded in particles of light at room temperature; used by Xanadu, PsiQuantum.

🔬 Neutral Atom

Arrays of atoms trapped by laser tweezers; highly scalable; used by QuEra, Pasqal.
